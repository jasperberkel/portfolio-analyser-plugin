# Market and candidate discovery

Adapted from sector-overview, idea-generation and competitive-analysis; see ../anthropic-sources.md.

Map relevant macro/sector developments, value chains, competing business models and economic drivers across listed stocks, retail-accessible ETFs/ETCs, major crypto and commodities through accessible instruments. Search beyond current headlines and dominant themes. The scope is general and must not be inferred from holdings, earlier portfolio advice or personal constraints.

Select news from any topic by its plausible impact on markets. Check fresh sources for new developments and for changes to the last recorded facts, including resolutions. Use the previous market context even if it predates the named changes section. A later article repeating an unchanged fact is not a development.

In the changes section, give each new or materially changed fact with its date and direct source link (also record the source in `research_data.sources`). For updates, say what changed from the previous observation. Explain which markets could be affected and how. Distinguish the observed fact from the inferred effect. Include material news even when it yields no candidate.

On the first run, identify the missing comparison baseline and record sourced current observations as the starting point. On a later run with no verified material change, state that briefly. Name material gaps if sources could not be checked. Outside the changes section, retain the last known dated facts and sources for developments still being followed.

Return at most eight identified, plausible candidates with a preliminary hypothesis, sourced rationale, risks and specific questions for instrument research. Check exact share class/fund identity and basic retail accessibility. Put unresolved identities in deferred; do not invent identifiers. A candidate is an investigation proposal, not an attractiveness judgment. Zero candidates is a successful outcome.

Where comparable structured evidence is available, supplement open macro/sector discovery with reproducible asset-appropriate screening using [structured tools](structured-tools.md). Report the explicit universe and assessed coverage. Favor long-term operating, balance-sheet, valuation or product-economics questions. Pure price/volume breakouts are not investment theses. Do not claim whole-market coverage from a bounded dataset, or treat unavailable facts as failed criteria.

For each retained candidate add `review_trigger`: the concrete evidence or unanswered question that warrants another investigation. `review_after` may be an evidenced future timestamp, otherwise null/omitted. It is a research reminder in the candidate record, not a monitoring subscription or trade date.
