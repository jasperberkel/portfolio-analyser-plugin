# Market and candidate discovery

Adapted from sector-overview, idea-generation and competitive-analysis; see ../anthropic-sources.md.

Map relevant macro/sector developments, value chains, competing business models and economic drivers across listed stocks, retail-accessible ETFs/ETCs, major crypto and commodities through accessible instruments. Search beyond current headlines and dominant themes. The scope is general and must not be inferred from holdings, earlier portfolio advice or personal constraints.

Return at most eight identified, plausible candidates with a preliminary hypothesis, sourced rationale, risks and specific questions for instrument research. Check exact share class/fund identity and basic retail accessibility. Put unresolved identities in deferred; do not invent identifiers. A candidate is an investigation proposal, not an attractiveness judgment. Zero candidates is a successful outcome.
