# Valuation and adversarial thesis review

Adapted from comps-analysis and thesis-tracker; see ../anthropic-sources.md.

Apply the same evidence standards to held instruments and candidates. Keep business/protocol health, valuation and thesis change separate. Select peers by economics, size/stage and business mix; justify material exclusions. Use comparable pricing timestamps, currencies, share classes, periods and accounting bases. Do not force P/E for losses, corporate multiples onto tokens, or an undervaluation claim for a diversified fund's useful structural role.

State an evidence-based counterargument, the reason against immediate change, claim-specific confidence and an observable invalidation condition. Reopen critical evidence and investigate contradictions directly when necessary. A second worker is not proof of independence. Lack of pricing or comparable estimates means unassessed valuation; it does not erase independently established risk.

For a new thesis generate a UUID once as described in the V2 contract. Track candidate hypotheses even when the conclusion is uncertain. Preserve all supplied thesis IDs, original first_seen_at and last substantive changed_at. Mark disproven theses invalidated rather than deleting them. The first structured review creates a research baseline, not an invented original purchase motivation. Changed citations alone do not reset a thesis date.

No allocation targets, trades or automatic DCF. If no candidate survives, say so. No loop asking the radar to keep generating names.
