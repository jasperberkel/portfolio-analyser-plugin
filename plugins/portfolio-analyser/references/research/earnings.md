# Earnings review and preview

Adapted from Anthropic earnings-analysis/earnings-preview; see ../anthropic-sources.md.

For new results compare revenue, margins, cash flow, segments, balance-sheet liquidity and guidance against correctly labelled prior expectations. Explain the operating drivers rather than listing beats/misses. Choose metrics appropriate to the business; unprofitable businesses require runway and dilution analysis. Compare like periods, accounting definitions and currencies. Half-year reporters are valid; do not impose a quarterly freshness rule.

For upcoming confirmed results name the few metrics that could support or weaken the thesis, with conditional scenarios and a cited date. Do not invent consensus, whisper numbers, options signals, ratings, price targets or expected price reactions. Guidance comparisons must say guidance. Historical consensus needs evidence of the estimate before the announcement.

Return compact facts and source-backed events, not Word/Excel deliverables. If nothing new occurred, retain the fact period and event dates instead of presenting the same result as fresh news.
