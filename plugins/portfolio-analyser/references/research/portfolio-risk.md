# Portfolio structure and risk

Selected concepts adapted from portfolio-rebalance/client-review; see ../anthropic-sources.md. Calculations and scope rules are our own.

Use supplied calculations unchanged. Explain direct and evidenced indirect concentration and fund overlap with the composition date. The rows combine fund positions and their underlyings: do not sum them as disjoint allocations. Incomplete holdings lists leave residual exposure unknown. Dated look-through is an estimate, not live exposure. Foreign-currency amounts are not silently added to base-currency amounts.

Inspect evidenced asset/country/sector/currency factors, liquidity and product structure. Compare only explicit, unambiguous existing plan targets; do not invent targets from prose. When a numeric drift calculation is needed, save its inputs and compute with decimal arithmetic. Mark missing country/sector/look-through or FX data.

No new allocation plan, tax lots, transaction history, external wealth, correlation, VaR or performance attribution without the required data. Portfolio strategy owns the personal decision.
